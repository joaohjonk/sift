# Sift · Writing system

1 Oct 2026. Applies to every line on sift.cc, in the deck and in product UI. Sits under BRAND.md §07; when they disagree, BRAND wins. Rewrites of every live key: `rewrite.json`.

## 1 · No slop

Each pattern below is what a model writes when it has nothing to say. Kill it on sight.

| Pattern | Slop | Sift |
|---|---|---|
| The world opener | In a world crowded with choices, choosing well is rare. | Anyone can buy anything now, so we follow the people who choose well. |
| Not X but Y | It's not a link in bio, it's a record. | Her posts stay as they are. The links live on the page. |
| Colon as punch line | Two faces, one engine: Page and Record. | A page for the people who follow her. A record for the people who pay her. |
| Reflex triplet | Discover, curate, share. | Swipe right to keep, left to let go. |
| Abstract verb on an abstract noun | Finding what's worth it is broken. | There has never been more to buy, or less help choosing. |
| One verb everywhere | moves the world, moves millions, what they move, moves money | One "moves" per page. Then sway, sell, reach. |
| SaaS helper voice | We help you sift through it. | Sift shows what they kept, and what they left out. |
| Inflated stakes | the rarest skill there is | very few people are good at it |
| Definition template | Sift Page is what everyone sees. | Sift Page shows what she kept, with the links. |
| Hedge and adverb | We simply aim to really help. | Delete both words. |
| Synonym cycling | page, vitrine, shop, rolodex for one thing | One name per thing: Sift Page, Sift Record, Monte o look. |
| Dash as drama | Taste — finally on record. | Taste, on record. |

Also banned: delve, unlock, elevate, seamless, curated as praise, journey, game changer, empower, "it's worth noting", any exclamation mark, any emoji.

## 2 · The tests

Every line passes all six, or it goes back.

1. **See it.** Can a reader picture it? A coat, a thousand buyers, day 52. (Harry Dry)
2. **Falsify it.** Could it be proved wrong? "Six of 214" can; "the best taste" can't. (Harry Dry)
3. **Only us.** Could Glossier, ShopMy or a bank print it unchanged? Then it isn't ours. (Harry Dry)
4. **Zero context.** A stranger on a phone, no deck, gets it on the first read.
5. **Read aloud.** If it only sounds right in your head, or only in English, rewrite it from the idea.
6. **Count the cost.** Every word in UI is a tap of friction. Cut until it breaks, then put one back. (Nikita Bier, Julian Shapiro)

## 3 · English

- Clear first, editorial second. A 032c sentence a Vignelli manual would sign off.
- Short declaratives with a concrete noun in each. Numbers beat adjectives.
- Headline and standfirst are one unit and never repeat each other. The hed claims, the dek proves.
- Captions credit the maker and state the fact: `Kept · No. 068 · Mansur Gavriel, box bags`.
- She, her. The page is about one chooser.
- Contractions are fine. Exclamation marks, dashes and hype are not.
- World words carry the brand: keep, kept, left out, on record, sift. Spend them; don't explain them.
- Fragments only where the form wants them: labels, buttons, the closing "On purpose."
- Monocle's rule: optimism with a fact under it. Never a complaint without the fix.

## 4 · Português

- Escreva direto em português, a partir da ideia. Se a frase só funciona quando você remonta o inglês na cabeça, jogue o esqueleto fora e recomece.
- Registro de revista boa, não de agência: a frase da piauí, o olhar da Amarello, a gíria do meio usada com parcimônia (publi, recebido, job, arara).
- Uma coisa, um nome. É **Sift Page**, **Sift Record** e **Monte o look**. "Vitrine" vende loja e fica fora.
- Decalque é defeito, não sotaque: "deixar ir" (let go), "não parece certo" (doesn't look right), "faz sentido" como elogio, "no final do dia", "Num mundo de...".
- Nada de "não é X, é Y" nem dois-pontos guardando o golpe final. Diga a relação: "a diferença é que", "por isso".
- Concordância vem do substantivo em português. Adjetivo da leitura de estilo é feminino porque concorda com "escolha" ou "edição".
- "Você" na interface, "a gente" quando a Sift fala de si num tom de conversa. "Pra" só quando a frase inteira for de conversa.
- Ironia leve é bem-vinda: "pouca coisa que preste", "nenhum recibo". Lição de moral, nunca.

## 5 · Reference sentences

Imitate the move, not the words.

1. Someone you trust says one word, and a thousand people buy the same coat.
2. 214 things in one feed. Six worth keeping.
3. The brand sees what sold. The agency sees every deal. She sees all of it.
4. Left out: the sunglasses and the bike. Still on record.
5. Kept {k} of {n}. The rest went back.
6. Bom gosto merece nome e sobrenome.
7. Comprar ficou fácil. Difícil é escolher, e pouca gente tem mão para isso.
8. Cada publi, com contrato e recibo.
9. Por enquanto, é pouca gente. De propósito.
