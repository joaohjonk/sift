# Sift · Copy system

Source of truth for every word, 1 Oct 2026, from JJ. It sits above WRITING.md (the no-slop toolkit) and next to BRAND.md. Applied to site/assets/i18n.js on 1 Oct. Live copy never uses dashes, so the em dashes in the drafts became commas or full stops.

**The sentence that judges every next decision.** Sift is where a choice gets an author, a history and a consequence. (PT: A Sift é onde a escolha passa a ter autor, histórico e consequência.)

## The central idea

Sift is about judgment becoming legible. There are infinite things to buy. Some people are unusually good at choosing. Other people already act on those choices. Sift records the choice, the omission, the source and the commercial consequence. The public product is taste. The private product is proof.

Three ideas, in order:
1. There is too much. Choosing well became valuable (the human problem).
2. Taste is choosing and leaving out (the cultural idea we own).
3. When that choice makes someone buy, there should be a record (the product and the business).

Progression: overchoice → judgment → trust → consequence → record → product → participation → money.

ShopMy says "Your Storefront. Your standard." and Benable says "real people, real recs". Sift does not compete in that vocabulary. Sift owns choice and consequence.

## The narrative, section by section

1. **Recognition.** There is too much to choose from. No creators, affiliate commerce or infrastructure yet.
2. **Judgment.** Taste is not liking things. It becomes visible when someone has to choose. Selection and omission weigh the same.
3. **Consequence.** People already buy on other people's judgment. When one person's judgment changes what thousands buy, it has economic consequence.
4. **Record.** The missing layer is provenance. Who chose it, who rejected it, who caused the sale, what deal sat behind it, who gets paid.
5. **Product.** Sift Page is the public edit. Sift Record is the private proof. Never generic creator tools.
6. **Participation.** The games demonstrate the thesis: you cannot reveal taste without constraints. Choosing means leaving things out.
7. **Economics.** If your judgment already makes things sell, Sift gives that effect a record. Creator, agency and brand conversion belong here.
8. **Access.** Finish quietly. No growth hacking, no manufactured exclusivity.

## Verbal territory

**English owns:** keep, kept, left out, choose, edit, source, page, record, proof, trace, on record. "Creator" for the commercial actor, "people you trust" for the cultural behaviour. Voice: editorial, restrained, precise, slightly dry, institutional without sounding corporate. A caption, a marginal note, an archival record.

**Português owns:** fica, ficou, sai, de fora, escolha, fonte, página, registro, prova, nome e sobrenome. More oral than the English. Shorter clauses, better cadence, the occasional Brazilian turn of phrase, no English syntax. Wit, never performed cleverness.

**Avoid:** curated, authentic, community, empower, unlock, discover, seamless, ecosystem, platform for, monetize your taste, creator economy, influence at scale, AI-powered, shopping destination. In PT also: curadoria, autêntico, jornada, ecossistema, potencializar, descoberta, experiência personalizada, monetizar seu gosto. Never translate the name: no "peneirar". Never call the Sift Page a storefront, vitrine or rolodex.

**Sift is a verb (JJ, 1 Oct).** To sift is to choose well and leave the rest out. Use it as a verb so the name becomes the act, the way people google or venmo: "Sift around and build your look", "Sift again", "Sift this post". In PT the verb is borrowed: "dar um sift", "dê um sift", "dar outro sift" (and "siftar" once it catches on), never "peneirar". Use it in action moments (toys, buttons, shares), not to define the company.

## Writing laws

1. One thought per sentence.
2. Physical nouns and observable actions over abstractions.
3. If the sentence could appear on ShopMy, LTK, Pinterest or Benable, rewrite it.
4. Selection and rejection appear throughout the language.
5. The Sift Page is never a storefront or vitrine.
6. Product instructions are literal. Brand writing can be poetic. Never swap those jobs.
7. Headlines create tension. Subheads resolve it.
8. Never repeat the thesis in slightly different words right under itself.
9. One word per product concept. Repetition creates ownership.
10. English and Portuguese express the same strategy, not the same sentences.
11. Never turn cultural observations into consultancy language.
12. The best Sift sentence feels obvious five seconds after reading it.

## The test

Can I picture it? Could reality prove it wrong? Could only Sift plausibly say it? Would a smart person say this aloud? Is there one word I can remove? If translated literally into the other language, did we probably do something wrong?
